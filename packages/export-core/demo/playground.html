Graph playground

Hover a node or an edge: highlight it and its neighbours. Drag a node: move it. Drag the background: pan. Wheel: zoom. Double-click: fit. Keys with the graph focused: + - 0 zoom and fit, arrows move the focus, Ctrl+arrows pan, Alt+Shift+arrows nudge the selected (or focused) node. Search: / or click the box, type, ↑ ↓, Enter. Click or drag in the minimap to move the view.
